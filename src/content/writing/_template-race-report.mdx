The headings below are shaped for a running race. For a ski race or a big
hike, keep the structure and change the nouns — the plan, what happened,
where it went wrong, what you would change.

## The plan

What you intended to do. Goal time, pacing plan, where you thought it would get hard.

## What happened

The actual race, in order. Aid stations are convenient section breaks. Be specific about
numbers — pace, temperature, how much you were eating per hour — because the specifics are
the only part anyone can learn from.

## Where it went wrong

Every race has this section, including the good ones. This is the part people read.

## What I'd change

Short. Two or three things, concrete enough to act on next time.
